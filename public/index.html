How it works

1 Make your Goodreads profile public: Settings → Privacy → "Anyone". Paste the profile URL above.
2 Find your library's key in the Libby app URL — e.g. libbyapp.com/library/nypl. Add it above.
3 Hit Check availability. Results stream in as each book is checked — available books appear first.
