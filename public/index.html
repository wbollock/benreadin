How it works

1 Put your books on Hardcover. You can bring them over from Goodreads in a few minutes. Make your library public, then enter your @username above.
2 Pick your library. Search by name, or use the code from your Libby link, like libbyapp.com/library/nypl.
3 Press Check my books. Books show up as they're checked, with the ones you can borrow now at the top.

benreadin isn't connected to Hardcover, Goodreads, Libby, OverDrive or Amazon. Library info comes live from OverDrive. As an Amazon Associate, benreadin may earn from qualifying purchases.
